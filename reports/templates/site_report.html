How to read this report

Every value carries its type. observed is measured directly; classified is the output of a rule or model; estimated is derived with stated uncertainty; derived is computed from other stored values; unavailable means the quantity could not be determined, and the reason is given rather than a number.

Indicator levels are indicators, not predictions. This system does not state that a dam will fail, or when. A blockage is reported as potential; depth and volume are reported only where they can be traced to a documented branch of the project's depth logic, and are otherwise marked unavailable with the reason.
